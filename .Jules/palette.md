## 2024-07-20 - Quick Actions Button Accessibility & Visibility
**Learning:** Icon-only toggle buttons hidden via opacity (e.g. `opacity-0 group-hover:opacity-100`) become inaccessible to keyboard users because tabbing into them does not make them visible. Furthermore, simple icon buttons need dynamic accessibility attributes (`aria-pressed` and updated `aria-label`) to communicate toggle state effectively to screen readers instead of just updating the `title`.
**Action:** When hiding interactive elements for aesthetic reasons (like quick actions on product cards), always apply `focus-within:opacity-100` alongside `group-hover:opacity-100` on the container. Additionally, provide `aria-pressed` based on toggle state and explicit `focus-visible:ring-2` on the buttons to assist keyboard navigation.
