## 2024-05-18 - Hover-Revealed Buttons Accessibility
**Learning:** Hover-revealed action button containers (like Quick Actions overlaying a product image) become inaccessible to keyboard-only users if they solely rely on `opacity-0 group-hover:opacity-100`. The buttons can receive focus, but they remain invisible. Furthermore, toggle buttons (like Favorite or Compare) often lack screen-reader context if they only have `title` and lack `aria-pressed`.
**Action:** When using `group-hover:opacity-100` to reveal interactive elements, always pair it with `focus-within:opacity-100` on the container. Ensure toggle buttons use `aria-pressed` to correctly announce their active state, add `aria-label` when there's no visible text, and explicitly provide `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:outline-none` for visible keyboard focus states.
