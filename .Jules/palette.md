## 2026-04-22 - Toggle Buttons and Hidden Action Accessibility
**Learning:** Hidden quick-action containers (`opacity-0 group-hover:opacity-100`) render their contents inaccessible to keyboard users unless `focus-within:opacity-100` is applied. Furthermore, icon-only toggle buttons (like favorites or compare) require `aria-pressed` to correctly announce their boolean state to screen readers instead of relying on `title` attributes alone. Dynamic state changes in buttons (like "Add to Cart" changing to "Added") require `aria-live="polite"` to announce the update naturally.
**Action:** Always pair hover-based visibility classes with `focus-within:opacity-100` on the parent container. Add `aria-pressed`, `aria-label`, and `focus-visible:ring-2` to icon-only toggle buttons, and use `aria-live="polite"` for dynamic inline button state changes.
