## 2026-04-22 - Improving Keyboard Accessibility in Arabic E-Commerce UI
**Learning:** Hidden hover-based "Quick Action" buttons (favorite, compare) inside product cards are inaccessible to keyboard users unless explicitly handled. Adding `focus-within:opacity-100` alongside hover states is crucial for visibility. Also, adding `aria-live` to dynamic feedback buttons (like 'Add to Cart' changing to 'Added') ensures screen readers accurately announce the state change in localized apps without needing full focus shifts.
**Action:** Always pair `opacity-0 group-hover:opacity-100` with `focus-within:opacity-100` on hidden actionable containers. Always apply explicit focus rings (`focus-visible:ring-2`) and `aria-pressed` states to toggleable icon buttons, ensuring screen reader users can identify the current toggle state.
