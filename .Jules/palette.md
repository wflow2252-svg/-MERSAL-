## 2024-05-18 - Keyboard Navigation & Dynamic Button State Announcements
**Learning:** Hidden interactive elements (like quick action buttons that appear on hover) create an accessibility barrier because keyboard-only users cannot see where focus is when they tab to them. Furthermore, dynamic buttons that confirm an action (e.g., Add to Cart briefly turning green and showing "تمت الإضافة") may not be noticed by screen reader users without appropriate ARIA attributes.
**Action:** Use `focus-within:opacity-100` on containers to ensure hidden actionable items become visible during keyboard navigation. Additionally, apply `aria-live="polite"` and `aria-atomic="true"` to dynamic buttons to ensure proper and natural screen reader announcements when their text content or state changes without interrupting other flows.
