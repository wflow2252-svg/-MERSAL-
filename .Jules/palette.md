## 2026-09-25 - Improve ProductCard accessibility\n**Learning:** Icon-only toggle buttons (like favorites and compare arrows) inside list components (like ProductCard) require explicit aria-labels, aria-pressed states to communicate the active/inactive state, and visually explicit focus rings (`focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:outline-none`). When placed inside a container hidden on hover (`opacity-0 group-hover:opacity-100`), they also need `focus-within:opacity-100` so keyboard users can navigate to and see them.\n**Action:** Always add `focus-within` alongside `hover` visibility classes for interactive elements, and apply `aria-pressed` to toggle-based buttons.
