## 2024-03-24 - Accessibility for Hidden Quick Actions and Toggles
**Learning:** For quick action overlays that are typically hidden until hover (`group-hover:opacity-100`), they remain inaccessible to keyboard users unless explicitly revealed on focus. Additionally, toggle buttons (like "favorite" or "compare") need `aria-pressed` to correctly announce their active state to screen readers, rather than just relying on standard `aria-label` or `title` attributes.
**Action:** Always add `focus-within:opacity-100` alongside `group-hover:opacity-100` on containers of interactive elements. For toggle actions, ensure `aria-pressed` is dynamically set based on the state variable to convey boolean states properly, and provide distinct `focus-visible` styling (like `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:outline-none`).
