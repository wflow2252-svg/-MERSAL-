## 2026-08-05 - Enhance ProductCard Quick Actions and Add to Cart Buttons
**Learning:** Adding `focus-within:opacity-100` alongside `group-hover:opacity-100` on hidden icon button containers ensures they become visible during keyboard navigation without breaking standard hover designs. Utilizing `aria-pressed` for toggle states (like favorites or comparing products) and explicitly mapping `focus-visible` styles enhances screen reader visibility and keyboard user experience. Also, state changes triggered by "Add to Cart" should include `aria-live="polite"` and `aria-atomic="true"` for smooth announcements.
**Action:** When implementing hidden quick actions (e.g., hover effects) or togglable icon buttons in Arabic/RTL design, verify focus-within combinations and ensure the presence of clear ARIA and `focus-visible` states to improve overall accessibility.
